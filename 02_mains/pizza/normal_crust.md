# WIP Pizza Crust (Dough Recipie)
A chewy, 12-inch crust for a standard home oven.\
Makes 1 twelve-inch crust.

### Ingredients:
- 1 1/3 cups bread flour (spooned and leveled, not scooped)
- 1/2 cup warm water (~105ºF)
- 1/2 tsp instant yeast
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1 tsp olive oil (plus extra for the bowl)
- 1/2 tsp sugar (optional, helps browning)

### Supplies:
- Large mixing bowl
- Wooden spoon
- Plastic wrap or a damp towel
- Pizza stone, steel, or an inverted heavy baking sheet
- Parchment paper
- Rolling pin (not recommended, but handy if you can't stretch by hand)

## Part 1: Making the dough (start ~2 hours before eating, or up to 3 days ahead)
1) In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, instant yeast, salt, and sugar.
2) Add the warm water and olive oil. Stir until a shaggy dough forms and no dry flour remains.
3) Knead on a lightly floured surface for 8–10 minutes (or ~5 minutes in a stand mixer with a dough hook) until smooth, elastic, and slightly tacky. It should pass the windowpane test: stretch a piece thin enough to see light through without tearing.
4) Shape the dough into a ball, place it in a lightly oiled bowl, and turn to coat.

## Part 2: Rising (pick one)
*Note: The room-temp rise and the fridge are two paths to the same place, not both. Same-day pizza = room temp. Make-ahead or deeper flavor = fridge.
- **Same day:** Cover and let rise at room temperature until doubled, about 1–2 hours.
- **Make ahead (recommended for flavor):** Seal the dough ball in a lightly oiled container or zip-top bag and refrigerate for 24–72 hours. When ready to bake, take it out and let it sit at room temperature for 30–60 minutes until it loses its chill.

## Part 3: Shaping and baking (start ~1 hour before eating)
1) Place a pizza stone, steel, or inverted baking sheet on the middle rack. Preheat the oven as hot as it goes (ideally 500–550ºF) for at least 45 minutes.
2) Punch the dough down, shape it into a tight ball, and let it rest covered for 10–15 minutes so the gluten relaxes.
3) On a floured surface or a sheet of parchment, press and stretch the dough into a 12-inch round, leaving a slightly thicker rim. Avoid a rolling pin if you can, since it presses out the air bubbles.
4) Top lightly with sauce, cheese, and toppings so the crust can crisp.
5) Slide the pizza (on its parchment) onto the hot surface and bake for 8–12 minutes, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbling.
6) Let it cool a couple of minutes before slicing.
