# Pizza Sauce (Fresh Tomato)
A simple cooked tomato sauce for pizza.\
Makes about 1/2 cup (enough for one 12-inch pizza).

### Ingredients:
- 3 medium ripe tomatoes (or about 1/2 cup canned crushed tomatoes, see note)
- 1 clove garlic, minced (or 2 for a garlicky sauce)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp salt (plus more to taste)

### Supplies:
- Small saucepan
- Wooden spoon
- Cutting board and knife (or a box grater)
- Sieve (only for the no-cook version)

## Part 1: Making the sauce (start ~25 minutes before topping the pizza)
1) Core and roughly chop the tomatoes. For a shorter less watery sauce, scoop out and discard most of the seeds first. (For a smoother result, grate the tomatoes on a box grater and discard the skins instead of chopping.)
2) Heat the olive oil in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant but not browned, about 30–60 seconds.
3) Add the chopped tomatoes, oregano, and salt. Stir to combine and bring to a gentle simmer.
4) Simmer over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until thickened and most of the liquid has cooked off, about 15–20 minutes. You want a spreadable, not watery, sauce so the crust stays crisp — it should mound on the spoon, not run off.
5) Mash with a spoon for a rustic texture, or blend for a smooth sauce. Taste and add more salt if needed, then let it cool slightly.
6) Spread about 1/3–1/2 cup over the 12-inch dough, leaving a border around the edge.

*Note: Canned shortcut: canned tomatoes are already concentrated, so skip most of the reduction. Just warm about 1/2 cup of crushed tomatoes with the garlic, oil, oregano, and salt for ~5 minutes.

*Note: No-cook version: for a brighter, Neapolitan-style sauce, skip the stove entirely. Blend (or mash) the raw tomatoes with the garlic, oil, oregano, and salt, then drain off excess liquid in a sieve before spreading.
