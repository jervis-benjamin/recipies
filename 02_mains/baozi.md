# Baozi
Steamed buns.
Makes 5 buns.

### Ingredients:

**Dough:**
- 2 cups all-purpose flour (can also do a 1:1 all purpose to whole wheat ratio, just add 7/8 cup of water instead of 3/4)
- 3/4 cup warm water (additonaly 3/4 cup for active dry yeast activation)
- 1 tsp instant yeast (or active dry yeast, see below)
- 1 tbsp sugar (only if using active dry yeast)
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tsp neutral oil (optional, for softer dough)

**Filling:**\
*Note: This is just a modified samosa filling that somehow worked (since I didn't have any meat at the time). I will list the filling steps here, but feel free to use any filling you want.
- 1 cup finely chopped cabbage
- 1/2 cup grated carrot
- 1/2 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 small tomato, with seeds and chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 2–3 green onions, finely chopped
- 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/4 tsp turmeric
- 1/4–1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste
- 1 tbsp oil

### Supplies:
- Large mixing bowl
- Steamer basket and pot with lid
- Parchment paper or cabbage/lettuce leaves (to prevent the buns from sticking to the steamer)
- Rolling pin

## Part 1: Making the dough (start ~2 hours before eating)
1) If using active dry yeast, dissolve it with the sugar in about 3/4 cup warm water and wait 5–10 minutes until foamy. If using instant yeast, skip this step and mix everything together directly.
2) Combine the flour, salt, yeast mixture (or instant yeast), and warm water. Mix until a shaggy dough forms, then knead for 8–10 minutes, or until the dough is smooth and soft.
3) Cover the bowl and let the dough rise for 1 hour, or until doubled in size.

## Part 2: Making the filling
1) Cover salt around cabbage leaves and let it sit for 5 minutes, then squeeze out as much water as possible.
2) Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle for 30 seconds.
3) Add the onion and cook until softened. Add garlic and ginger and cook for 30 more seconds.
4) Add ground cumin, coriander, turmeric, and chili powder. Stir briefly.
5) Add the tomato and cook until it breaks down, about 2 minutes.
6) Add the cabbage and carrot and stir-fry until the mixture is dry, about 5–7 minutes. No liquid should remain.
7) Turn off the heat. Stir in the green onions and garam masala. Adjust salt to taste.
8) Let the filling cool completely before assembling.

## Part 3: Assembling and steaming
1) Punch down the risen dough and divide it into 5 equal pieces. Roll each into a ball.
2) Flatten each ball and roll into a 4–5 inch circle, keeping the center slightly thicker than the edges.
3) Place 2–3 tablespoons of filling in the center of each wrapper. Pleat the edges together and pinch tightly to seal.
4) Place the buns on parchment paper or cabbage/lettuce leaves in the steamer. Cover and let them rest for 15–20 minutes.
5) Bring the water in the pot to a boil, then steam the buns on medium heat for 15 minutes.
6) Turn off the heat and wait 3 minutes before opening the lid (prevents the buns from collapsing).
