# Boule
A crusty round loaf, with a dutch oven from https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OI2-6Ps2Hcc.\
Makes 1 loaf.

### Ingredients:
- 2 teaspoons of salt
- 1/8 teaspoon dry yeast (two small pinches)
- Around 5-6 cups of bread flour
- A little wheat flour

### Supplies:
- Large mixing bowl
- Wooden spoon
- Cloth/towel for covering the dough while proofing
- Parchment paper
- Bowl for proofing
- Dutch oven with lid

## Part 1: Making the poolish (do this the night before you bake)
1) Combine 1 cup of water with the yeast and enough bread and wheat flour to get a thick batter consistency. For every 1 part of bread flour, add around 20% wheat flour (adjustable to your preference).
2) Mix with a wooden spoon, leave it in the bowl, cover the poolish, and let it ferment for 8-24 hours.

## Part 2: Forming the dough (start around 4 hours before baking)
1) Uncover the poolish and mix 1 cup of water, salt, and as much bread flour as you can with the spoon. Cover and let it rest for an hour.
2) Uncover again and mix in a little more flour. When you are done, take out the spoon and cover and rest for one more hour.
3) Sprinkle the dough with some flour and picture the dough as if its a square. Grab one side of it and pull it out just before it tears and fold it back on itself. Repeat this for all 4 sides of the "square." Once done, position the dough so the seems are on the bottom. Cover and let it rest for 20 min.
4) Repeat step 3, let it rest for 20 min.
5) While the dough is resting, crumple a sheet of parchment paper and stuff it down the bottom of the dutch oven such that the bottom and parts of the sides are covered. 
6) Once 20 min from step 4 are done, repeat step 3 again, and place the dough in the dutch oven with the seams in the bottom. This time, let it rest covered for 30 minutes.

## Part 3: Baking
1) While the dough is resting, pre-heat the oven to 450ºF and put the lid of the dutch oven in the regular oven to heat up.
2) Once 30 min are up, score the surface of the dough, put the lid back on, and transfer it to the oven and let it bake for ~30 min.
3) Remove the lid (carefully) and let the bread bake for around 15 more minutes (until the surface of the bread becomes a rich orange-brown color).
4) Take the bread out of the oven and the dutch oven, and let it cool on a cooling rack (or any elevated platforms with gaps for air) before slicing.
