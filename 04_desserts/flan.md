# Flan
Crème brûlée's cousin.\
Makes 4, 1-cup ramekins of flan.

### Ingredients:
- 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
- 1 cup 2% milk
- 4 eggs
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon (optional)
- 1 cup sugar

### Supplies:
- 1 pan for caramel making
- 4 ramekins
- 1 big oven safe baking dish to hold ramekins
- 2 mixing bowls
- Filter/sieve

## Part 1: Making the caramel
1) In a pot, add 1 cup of sugar with a little bit water (just enough to create a slurry).
2) Mix until the sugar and water are as uniform as can be (does not have to be perfect).
3) Turn the stove to medium-high heat and DO NOT MIX THE SUGAR FROM HERE ON OUT. Once you see bubbles forming, you may turn the stove to medium.
4) Closely observe the color. Once the mixture turns a slight yellow, turn the heat to medium-low, and have the ramekins on standby.
5) When the sugar is a orange, light-amber color, turn off the heat and pour an equal amount of sugar in each ramekin. ensure that sugar covers the bottom corners of the ramekins as well.
6) Set this aside and make the custard.

## Part 2: Making the custard
1) In a bowl, whisk 4 eggs.
2) Add the cinnamon, then mix till homogenous, and continue doing the same with the milk, condensed milk, then vanilla.
3) Strain the mixture into another bowl and continue 3-4 times until the custard is smooth and silky. 
4) Pour an equal amount of custard over the 4 ramekins.

## Part 3: Cooking
1) Place the ramekins into a baking dish.
2) Pour hot water into the dish until it reaches about halfway up the ramekins.
3) Bake at 350°F for roughly 1 hour.
4) If the flan is not set, bake again for an additional 25 min.
5) Take the ramekins outside the oven and water bath, and let it rest to room temperature.
6) Refrigerate for around 4+ hours, preferably overnight.

## Part 4: Serve
1) Warm the bottom of the ramekin with your hand or some warm water.
2) Run a knife around the edge of each ramekin and flip onto a plate.
